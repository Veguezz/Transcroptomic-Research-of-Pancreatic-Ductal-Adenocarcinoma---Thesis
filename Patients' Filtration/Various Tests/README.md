Description

Here, Kaplan-Meier method was used to compare the effect of age between "responders" and "not responders" with their overral survival time since entering the trail.

Then, Mann U Whitney was used again the effect of age between "responders" and "not responders" with their survival outcome.

Similarly, Fisher's exact test to compare the effect of other clinical information between "responders" and "not responders" with their survival outcome.

Finally, an evaluation of the mean and standard deviation of the patients' age per group.
